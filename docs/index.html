01The Firm

A CONSIDERED
APPROACH TO LEGAL WORK.

O. Abayomi & Associates is a full-service law practice in Sango, Ibadan — serving individuals, businesses and institutions across contentious and non-contentious matters.

Every instruction is handled by a named counsel, with the principal reviewing the file at each stage. We keep our advice plain, our fees transparent and our reporting timely.

The firm handles litigation and civil claims, corporate and commercial instructions, intellectual property consultancy, and structured dispute resolution through mediation and arbitration. The practice also provides corporate secretarial support and notarial services for clients who require them.

We are built for the long term. Our clients include individuals, families, small businesses, corporate entities and institutions across Oyo State and beyond — and our counsel is tailored to the commercial and personal reality of each instruction, not to a template.

Our approach is simple: understand the instruction, advise plainly, act decisively and report honestly. It is a standard we apply to a boundary dispute in Ibadan and a multi-party commercial negotiation alike.

05Dispute Resolution

WHEN A DISPUTE
NEEDS A DIFFERENT PATH.

Not every dispute belongs in court. We help parties choose the process that fits the commercial reality — and we litigate firmly when it does not.

01

Mediation

A structured, facilitated process in which parties work towards a mutually acceptable resolution with the support of a neutral third party.

02

Arbitration

A private adjudicative process where a neutral arbitrator considers the parties’ positions and issues a binding determination.

03

Litigation

When a negotiated outcome is not possible, we prepare and prosecute the case before the courts, from originating process to enforcement.
